## 🧠 Core Concepts

### Why Animals Need Food

- **Chemical energy** for ATP generation (cellular respiration)
- **Organic building blocks** for macromolecules (growth and repair)
- **Essential molecules** the body cannot synthesize

### The GI Tract (in order)

```
Mouth → Esophagus → Stomach → Small Intestine → Large Intestine → Rectum → Anus
```

### Processing at Each Stage

#### Mouth
- **Mechanical:** Teeth break food into smaller pieces → increases surface area
- **Chemical:** **Salivary amylase** begins starch digestion
- Swallowing → epiglottis closes trachea → bolus passes to esophagus

#### Stomach
- **Mechanical:** Muscular churning → chyme
- **Chemical:**
  - **Pepsin** (activated from pepsinogen by HCl) → protein digestion
  - Low pH (~2) denatures proteins and kills pathogens
- Output: Acidic chyme released into small intestine

#### Small Intestine (main absorption site)
- **Duodenum:** Receives bile (from liver/gallbladder) and pancreatic enzymes
  - **Bile** emulsifies fats (not an enzyme — mechanical)
  - **Pancreatic lipase** digests fats
  - **Pancreatic amylase** continues starch digestion
  - **Proteases** (trypsin, chymotrypsin) continue protein digestion
- **Jejunum/Ileum:** Absorption via villi and microvilli (brush border)

**Absorption:**
- **Carbohydrates:** As monosaccharides (glucose, fructose, galactose) → enter capillaries
- **Proteins:** As amino acids → enter capillaries
- **Fats:** As fatty acids + glycerol → reassembled into triglycerides → enter **lacteals** (lymph vessels) as chylomicrons

> ⚠️ **Key concept:** Nutrients are technically "outside the body" while they are in the GI lumen. They are only "inside the body" once absorbed across the intestinal epithelium into the blood or lymph.

#### Large Intestine (colon)
- Water reabsorption
- Houses gut microbiome
- Compacts undigested material into feces

### Weight Regulation
- **Leptin** is an important long-term signal for weight regulation
  - Produced by adipose tissue; signals fullness/satiety to the hypothalamus
