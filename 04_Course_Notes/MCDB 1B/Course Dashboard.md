## 🎯 Central Theme: Homeostasis

The unifying concept of this course is **homeostasis** — how organisms maintain a stable internal environment despite changing external conditions. Every system studied (nervous, muscular, circulatory, excretory, plant transport, etc.) is understood through the lens of homeostatic regulation.

**Parameters regulated for homeostasis:**
- Oxygen levels
- Temperature
- pH
- Osmolarity
- Nutritional molecules (glucose, fatty acids)
- Ion composition (Na⁺, K⁺, Cl⁻, Ca²⁺)
- Nitrogenous waste removal

## 🔬 Exam Format (Professor Kranzfelder)

- **Format:** Scantron — multiple choice, true/false, matching
- **Approach:** Concept application over rote memorization
- **Common traps:** Double negatives ("What is NOT…"), similar answer choices differing by direction of ion movement or feedback type
- **iClicker ConcepTESTs** from lecture appear as exam questions
- **Diagram-based questions** (label the neuron, trace blood flow, draw the nephron)
