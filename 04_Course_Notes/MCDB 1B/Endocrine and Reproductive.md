## 🧠 Core Concepts

### Modes of Chemical Signaling

| Mode | Where Signal Travels | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **Endocrine** | Blood → distant target cells | Insulin, estrogen |
| **Paracrine** | Local (nearby cells) | Prostaglandins |
| **Autocrine** | Same cell | Some growth factors |
| **Synaptic** | Neurotransmitter across synapse | Acetylcholine |

### Two Classes of Hormones

| Feature | **Steroid Hormones** | **Peptide/Protein Hormones** |
|---|---|---|
| **Chemistry** | Lipid-soluble (derived from cholesterol) | Water-soluble (amino acids) |
| **Receptor location** | Inside cell (nucleus or cytoplasm) | On cell surface |
| **Mechanism** | Diffuse through membrane → bind DNA → alter gene expression | Bind receptor → second messenger cascade |
| **Speed** | Slow (hours–days; changes gene expression) | Fast (seconds–minutes) |
| **Examples** | Cortisol, estradiol, testosterone, aldosterone | Insulin, glucagon, ADH, GH, epinephrine (amine) |

> ⚠️ **Anandamide** (the molecule THC mimics) is an **eicosanoid** — derived from fatty acids, acts as a local signal.

### Key Hormones

| Hormone | Source | Target | Function |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Insulin** | Pancreas (β cells) | Liver, muscle, adipose | Lowers blood glucose; promotes glucose uptake/storage |
| **Glucagon** | Pancreas (α cells) | Liver | Raises blood glucose; glycogen breakdown |
| **Cortisol** | Adrenal cortex | Many tissues | Stress response; suppresses immune response; gluconeogenesis |
| **Epinephrine (adrenaline)** | Adrenal medulla | Heart, vessels, liver | Fight-or-flight; rapid response |
| **ADH (vasopressin)** | Posterior pituitary | Kidney collecting ducts | Increases water reabsorption |
| **Growth Hormone (GH)** | Anterior pituitary | Liver, bones, muscles | Growth; metabolism |
| **Estradiol** | Ovary follicles | Uterus, hypothalamus | Female reproduction; secondary sex characteristics |
| **Testosterone** | Testes | Many tissues | Male reproduction; secondary sex characteristics |
| **LH** | Anterior pituitary | Ovary | Triggers ovulation; luteinizes follicle |
| **FSH** | Anterior pituitary | Ovary/testes | Follicle development; sperm production |

### Ovarian and Uterine Cycles (~28 days)

**Ovarian Cycle:**

| Days | Event | Hormones |
|---|---|---|
| 0–14 | Follicular phase: Follicle grows | FSH + LH → estradiol rises |
| ~14 | Ovulation | LH surge (triggered by rising estradiol) |
| 14–28 | Luteal phase: Corpus luteum forms | Progesterone + estradiol from corpus luteum |

**Uterine Cycle (endometrium):**
- Days 1–5: Menstruation (endometrium shed)
- Days 6–14: Proliferative phase (endometrium thickens, driven by estradiol)
- Days 15–28: Secretory phase (progesterone maintains endometrium for implantation)

**Feedback:**
- Rising estradiol (late follicular) → stimulates GnRH → LH surge → ovulation (positive feedback)
- Progesterone (luteal phase) → inhibits GnRH/LH/FSH (negative feedback)

### Reproductive Strategies

**Sexual reproduction:**
- Split sexes, hermaphrodites, sex reversal
- Increases genetic diversity

**Asexual reproduction:**
- Budding, fragmentation, parthenogenesis
- Faster; all offspring are genetic copies
