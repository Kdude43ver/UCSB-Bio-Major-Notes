# Nephron Function

## Definition
The functional unit of the kidney; performs filtration, reabsorption, secretion, and excretion to produce urine and maintain blood homeostasis.

## Mechanism (4 Steps)

### 1. Filtration
- Blood filtered at the **glomerulus** (capillary tuft)
- Pressure forces water, ions, glucose, urea, amino acids into **Bowman's capsule** → filtrate
- Large proteins and cells STAY in blood

### 2. Reabsorption
- Useful molecules returned FROM tubule lumen → blood
- **PCT:** ~65% of water, all glucose, amino acids, many ions reabsorbed (bulk reabsorption)
- **Loop of Henle:** Water reabsorbed (descending limb), ions reabsorbed (ascending limb)
- **DCT + Collecting Duct:** Fine-tuning under hormonal control

### 3. Secretion
- Additional wastes moved FROM peritubular capillaries → tubule lumen
- H⁺, NH₃, drugs, some ions → added to urine
- Important for pH regulation

### 4. Excretion
- Concentrated urine exits via collecting duct → ureter → bladder

## Loop of Henle (Counter-Current Multiplier)

| Segment | Permeability | What Moves? |
|---|---|---|
| **Descending limb** | Permeable to water; NOT ions | Water leaves (by osmosis into hyperosmotic medulla) |
| **Thin ascending limb** | Permeable to NaCl | NaCl diffuses out |
| **Thick ascending limb** | Pumps NaCl OUT; impermeable to water | NaCl actively pumped out → builds medullary gradient |

**Result:** Increasingly hyperosmotic from cortex → inner medulla → allows urine concentration.

## Hormonal Regulation

| Hormone | Site of Action | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| **ADH** | Collecting duct | Inserts aquaporins → water reabsorption → concentrated urine |
| **Aldosterone** | DCT + collecting duct | ↑ Na⁺ reabsorption → water follows → ↑ blood pressure |
| **ANP** | DCT | ↓ Na⁺ reabsorption → ↓ blood pressure |

## Biological Role
- Filters ~180 L of plasma/day; excretes ~1.5 L urine
- Regulates blood pressure, pH, ion composition, osmolarity
- Excretes nitrogenous wastes
