## Definition

Plant hormones (phytohormones) are chemical signals produced in small amounts that regulate plant growth, development, and responses to the environment. Unlike animal hormones, they often act locally and can be produced by many cell types.

## Mechanism

### The Five Classical Hormones

| Hormone | Produced by | Primary Effects | Key Examples |
|---------|-------------|----------------|--------------|
| **Auxin (IAA)** | Shoot apex, young leaves | Cell elongation; phototropism; gravitropism; apical dominance | Bending toward light; root-shoot gradient |
| **Gibberellins (GA)** | Young leaves, roots, seeds | Stem elongation; seed germination; fruit set; enzyme induction (α-amylase) | Dwarf plants lack GA; GA spray elongates stems |
| **Cytokinins** | Root tips | Cell division; delay senescence; lateral bud release | Work with auxin to control division |
| **Ethylene** | Ripening fruit, damaged tissue | Fruit ripening; leaf/fruit abscission; triple response; stress response | "One rotten apple spoils the barrel" |
| **ABA (abscisic acid)** | Leaves, roots (drought) | Stomatal closure; seed dormancy; stress response; inhibits germination | Drought → ABA → stomata close |

### Auxin in Detail
- **Phototropism**: Auxin migrates away from light → elongates shaded side → bends toward light
- **Gravitropism**: In roots, high auxin inhibits elongation → root grows downward
- **Polar transport**: Auxin moves unidirectionally from tip to base (via PIN efflux carriers)
- **Apical dominance**: Shoot tip auxin suppresses axillary buds; removal → branching

### Ethylene (gas hormone)
- **Triple response in dark**: horizontal growth, exaggerated apical hook, reduced elongation (response to soil obstruction)
- Ripening cascade: as one fruit ripens and releases ethylene → triggers neighbors → autocatalytic
- Controlled atmosphere storage: low O₂/low temperature slows ethylene production → delays ripening

### ABA (stress hormone)
- Drought → xylem pressure drop detected → ABA synthesis → guard cell signaling → stomata close
- ABA also promotes seed dormancy and inhibits germination
