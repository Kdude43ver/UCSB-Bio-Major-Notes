# Loop of Henle and Urine Concentration

**Course:** [[Course Dashboard]] | **Yield:** HIGH ⭐⭐⭐

---

## Definition

The loop of Henle is a hairpin-shaped segment of the nephron that creates a hypertonic medullary gradient via a countercurrent multiplier system. This gradient allows the collecting duct to concentrate urine under hormonal control.

## Mechanism

**Countercurrent multiplier:**

| Segment | Permeability | Action |
|---------|-------------|--------|
| Descending limb | Water permeable; solute impermeable | Water leaves by osmosis → filtrate becomes hypertonic |
| Ascending limb (thin) | Solute permeable | NaCl diffuses out passively |
| Ascending limb (thick) | Impermeable to water | NaCl actively pumped out (NKCC2) → filtrate becomes hypotonic |

**Medullary gradient:** highest osmolarity (≈1200 mOsm) at tip of loop; lowest at cortex.

**Collecting duct — hormonal control:**

| Hormone | Source | Effect on CD |
|---------|--------|-------------|
| **ADH** (antidiuretic hormone / vasopressin) | Posterior pituitary | Inserts aquaporin-2 channels → water reabsorbed → concentrated urine |
| **Aldosterone** | Adrenal cortex | Increases Na⁺ channels/pumps in DCT/CD → Na⁺ (and water) reabsorbed |
| **ANP** (atrial natriuretic peptide) | Heart atria | Opposes aldosterone → Na⁺ (and water) excreted |

**Urea recycling:** Urea diffuses out of collecting duct into medulla, contributing ~450 mOsm to the gradient.

## Concept Relationships

- High ADH → aquaporins inserted → max water reabsorption → small volume, concentrated urine
- Low ADH (diabetes insipidus) → no aquaporins → large volume, dilute urine
- RAAS activation → ↑ aldosterone → ↑ Na⁺ reabsorption → ↑ blood pressure
