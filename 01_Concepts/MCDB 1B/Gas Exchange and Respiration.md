# Gas Exchange and Respiration

**Course:** [[Course Dashboard]] | **Yield:** MEDIUM ⭐⭐

---

## Definition

Gas exchange is the process by which organisms acquire O₂ for cellular respiration and eliminate CO₂. It occurs by passive diffusion driven by partial pressure gradients across respiratory surfaces. Different animals have evolved diverse structures (gills, lungs, tracheal tubes) optimized for their environments.

## Mechanism

**Fick's Law of diffusion:**
Rate of diffusion ∝ (Surface area × Concentration gradient × Solubility) / (Membrane thickness × √Molecular weight)

**Key variables:**
- ↑ Surface area → ↑ gas exchange (lungs: ~70 m² in humans)
- ↑ Concentration (partial pressure) gradient → ↑ diffusion rate
- Thin, moist membrane → essential for gas diffusion

### Mammalian Lung

**Pathway:** Nasal cavity → trachea → bronchi → bronchioles → alveoli

**Alveolus:**
- Epithelial cells (type I: gas exchange; type II: surfactant production)
- Surfactant (phospholipids) reduces surface tension → prevents collapse
- Surrounded by capillaries (one-cell thick)

**Ventilation mechanics:**
- **Inspiration**: diaphragm contracts → thoracic volume ↑ → pressure ↓ → air flows in
- **Expiration**: diaphragm relaxes → elastic recoil → air expelled (quiet breathing passive)

**Partial pressures (normal):**
| Gas | Atmospheric | Alveoli | Arterial blood | Venous blood | Tissues |
|----|-------------|---------|----------------|-------------|---------|
| PO₂ | 160 mmHg | 100 mmHg | 100 mmHg | 40 mmHg | 40 mmHg |
| PCO₂ | 0.3 mmHg | 40 mmHg | 40 mmHg | 46 mmHg | 46 mmHg |

### Comparative Respiratory Systems
| System | Example | Feature |
|--------|---------|---------|
| Gills (counter-current) | Fish | Water flows opposite to blood → maximizes O₂ extraction |
| Book lungs | Spiders | Stacked lamellae |
| Tracheal system | Insects | Air tubes direct to cells; no blood transport |
| Lungs | Vertebrates | Ventilation + circulatory delivery |
