# Phytochrome

## Definition
A photoreceptor pigment in plants that detects red and far-red light, existing in two interconvertible forms (Pr and Pfr) to regulate developmental responses to light.

## Mechanism

| Form | Absorbs | Converts to | State |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Pr** (red-absorbing) | Red light (660 nm) | Pfr | Inactive; stable in dark |
| **Pfr** (far-red-absorbing) | Far-red light (730 nm) | Pr | Active; promotes responses |

**Conversions:**
- Red light → Pr **converts to Pfr** (active)
- Far-red light → Pfr **converts back to Pr** (inactive)
- In darkness → Pfr slowly converts to Pr over time

**Key rule:** The **last light exposure** determines the final form and whether responses occur.

## Problem-Solving Template
*"Red light, then far-red light, then red light — what form? Will seeds germinate?"*
1. Red → converts to Pfr
2. Far-red → converts back to Pr
3. Red → converts to Pfr ← **final form**
**Pfr = active → YES, seeds germinate**

## Biological Responses Mediated by Pfr (active form)
- Seed germination
- Seedling de-etiolation (straightening, leaf expansion)
- Shade avoidance responses
- Flowering (in some species)

## Key Components
- **Chromophore:** Light-absorbing portion (phytochromobilin)
- **Protein:** Apo-protein that changes conformation with chromophore

## Biological Role
- Allows plants to detect day length (photoperiodism)
- Enables shade avoidance behavior
- Regulates germination timing relative to light conditions
