# Steroid vs Peptide Hormones

**Course:** [[Course Dashboard]] | **Yield:** HIGH ⭐⭐⭐

---

## Definition

Hormones are chemical messengers released into the bloodstream that act on distant target cells. They fall into two main classes — steroid hormones (lipid-soluble) and peptide/protein hormones (water-soluble) — with fundamentally different mechanisms of action.

## Mechanism

### Comparison Table

| Property | Steroid Hormones | Peptide/Protein Hormones |
|----------|-----------------|-------------------------|
| **Chemical nature** | Lipid-soluble (cholesterol-derived) | Water-soluble (amino acid chains) |
| **Receptor location** | Intracellular (cytoplasm or nucleus) | Cell surface (transmembrane receptors) |
| **Mechanism** | Direct gene regulation (transcription factors) | Second messenger cascades (cAMP, IP₃, DAG) |
| **Speed** | Slow (hours to days) | Fast (seconds to minutes) |
| **Duration** | Long-lasting | Short-lived |
| **Transport** | Requires carrier proteins in blood | Dissolved freely |
| **Examples** | Cortisol, estrogen, testosterone, aldosterone | Insulin, glucagon, ADH, LH, FSH, GH |

### Steroid Mechanism
1. Hormone diffuses through plasma membrane
2. Binds intracellular receptor → hormone-receptor complex forms
3. Complex translocates to nucleus → binds HRE (hormone response element) on DNA
4. Activates or represses transcription of target genes

### Peptide Mechanism (cAMP example)
1. Hormone binds GPCR on cell surface
2. G protein activates adenylyl cyclase → ATP → cAMP
3. cAMP activates PKA → phosphorylates target proteins
4. Rapid cellular response (seconds)

## Key Hormones

| Hormone | Class | Gland | Primary Target | Effect |
|---------|-------|-------|----------------|--------|
| Insulin | Peptide | Pancreas (β) | Liver, muscle, fat | ↓ blood glucose |
| Glucagon | Peptide | Pancreas (α) | Liver | ↑ blood glucose |
| Cortisol | Steroid | Adrenal cortex | Widespread | Stress; ↑ glucose; ↓ immune |
| Aldosterone | Steroid | Adrenal cortex | Kidney DCT/CD | ↑ Na⁺ reabsorption |
| ADH | Peptide | Posterior pituitary | Kidney CD | ↑ water reabsorption |
| Estradiol | Steroid | Ovary | Uterus, bone | Female secondary sex chars |
| Testosterone | Steroid | Testes | Widespread | Male secondary sex chars |
| LH | Peptide | Anterior pituitary | Gonads | Ovulation; testosterone |
