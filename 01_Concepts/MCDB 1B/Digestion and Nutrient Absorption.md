# Digestion and Nutrient Absorption

**Course:** [[Course Dashboard]] | **Yield:** MEDIUM ⭐⭐

---

## Definition

Digestion is the mechanical and chemical breakdown of food into absorbable molecules. Absorption occurs primarily in the small intestine, where nutrients cross the epithelium to enter the blood or lymph.

## Mechanism

### GI Tract Anatomy (mouth → anus)
Mouth → Esophagus → Stomach → Small intestine (duodenum → jejunum → ileum) → Large intestine → Rectum → Anus

**Accessory organs:** Liver (bile production), gallbladder (bile storage), pancreas (enzymes + bicarbonate)

### Chemical Digestion

| Nutrient | Enzyme | Source | Location |
|----------|--------|--------|----------|
| Starch | Salivary amylase | Salivary glands | Mouth |
| Starch | Pancreatic amylase | Pancreas | Duodenum |
| Proteins | Pepsin | Chief cells (stomach) | Stomach (pH 2) |
| Proteins | Trypsin, chymotrypsin | Pancreas | Duodenum |
| Triglycerides | Lipase | Pancreas | Duodenum |
| Lactose | Lactase | Small intestine villi | Small intestine |

**Bile:** Emulsifies fats (increases surface area for lipase); not an enzyme; produced by liver, stored in gallbladder, released into duodenum.

### Absorption

**Small intestine adaptations for absorption:**
- **Villi** → increase surface area
- **Microvilli** (brush border) → further ↑ surface area
- **Lacteals** (lymph capillaries in each villus) → absorb fat
- **Capillaries** in each villus → absorb glucose, amino acids, water-soluble vitamins

**Fat absorption pathway:**
- Triglycerides → fatty acids + monoglycerides (lipase)
- Absorbed into enterocytes → reassembled into triglycerides
- Packaged into **chylomicrons** (lipoprotein particles)
- Chylomicrons enter **lacteals** → lymph → thoracic duct → blood

**Carbohydrate/protein absorption:**
- Glucose, fructose, amino acids → absorbed into capillaries via portal vein → liver first-pass

### Hormonal Regulation
| Hormone | Trigger | Effect |
|---------|---------|--------|
| Gastrin | Food in stomach | ↑ HCl secretion |
| Secretin | Acid in duodenum | ↑ Bicarbonate from pancreas |
| CCK | Fat/protein in duodenum | ↑ Enzyme secretion; gallbladder contraction |
| Leptin | Fat cells (adipocytes) | ↓ Appetite; ↑ metabolism |
