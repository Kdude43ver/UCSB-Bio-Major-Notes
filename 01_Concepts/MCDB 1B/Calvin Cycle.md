# Calvin Cycle (Dark Reactions)

## Definition
The light-independent reactions of photosynthesis in which CO₂ is fixed into organic sugars using ATP and NADPH from the light reactions.

## Mechanism (3 Stages)

### Stage 1: Carbon Fixation
- CO₂ + RuBP (5C) → 2× 3-PGA (3C each)
- Enzyme: **RuBisCo**
- Location: Stroma

### Stage 2: Reduction
- 3-PGA + ATP + NADPH → G3P (glyceraldehyde-3-phosphate)
- Uses energy from light reactions
- G3P is the immediate product used to build glucose and other molecules

### Stage 3: Regeneration of RuBP
- G3P → RuBP (5C) using ATP
- Regenerates the CO₂ acceptor to keep the cycle running

## Net Input/Output (for 3 CO₂ fixed)
- **Input:** 3 CO₂, 9 ATP, 6 NADPH
- **Output:** 1 net G3P (rest used to regenerate RuBP)
- 2 G3P → 1 glucose (6 CO₂ needed per glucose)

## Key Components
- **RuBisCo:** Most abundant enzyme on Earth; fixes CO₂ but also reacts with O₂ (photorespiration)
- **RuBP:** 5-carbon CO₂ acceptor; regenerated each cycle
- **3-PGA:** 3-carbon intermediate
- **G3P:** 3-carbon sugar; building block for glucose

## Biological Role
- Converts inorganic CO₂ into organic compounds
- Feeds the rest of plant metabolism (sugars → starch, cellulose, lipids, amino acids)
- Provides sugars for phloem loading
