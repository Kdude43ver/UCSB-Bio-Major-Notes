# Nucleotide Excision Repair (NER)

**Topic Area:** DNA Repair
**Lecture:** [[DNA Repair]]
**Exam:** Final
**Importance:** HIGH YIELD ⭐⭐⭐

---

## One-Line Definition
NER removes **bulky, helix-distorting lesions** (UV pyrimidine dimers, chemical adducts) by excising a ~12–13 nucleotide oligonucleotide containing the damage, then filling the gap.

---

## Substrates
- UV-induced cyclobutane pyrimidine dimers (CPD) and 6-4 photoproducts
- Bulky chemical adducts (benzo[a]pyrene, cisplatin crosslinks)
- Any lesion that significantly distorts the double helix geometry

---

## UvrABC Mechanism (Prokaryotic)

| Step | Event | Proteins |
|------|-------|---------|
| 1 | UvrA₂B complex scans DNA; UvrA recognizes helix distortion | UvrA, UvrB |
| 2 | UvrB loads at damage site; UvrA₂ dissociates | UvrB |
| 3 | UvrC binds UvrB-DNA; makes two cuts: 8 nt **3'** and 4–5 nt **5'** to lesion | UvrC |
| 4 | ~12–13 nt oligonucleotide excised; UvrD displaces fragment + UvrC | UvrD |
| 5 | Gap filled using intact strand as template | DNA Pol I |
| 6 | Nick sealed | DNA Ligase |

**Memory:** UvrA finds, UvrB holds, UvrC cuts (dual incisions), UvrD displaces.

---

## Eukaryotic NER
- **Global Genome NER (GG-NER):** XPC-RAD23B senses helix distortion genome-wide
- **Transcription-Coupled NER (TC-NER):** Stalled RNAP II triggers preferential repair of the transcribed strand
- Excision patch in eukaryotes: **~25–30 nt** (larger than bacterial)

**Xeroderma Pigmentosum (XP):** Human genetic disease caused by NER deficiency; extreme UV sensitivity, >1000× increased skin cancer risk. Seven complementation groups (XPA–XPG), each corresponding to a different NER factor.

---

## Comparison to BER
| Feature | NER | BER |
|---------|-----|-----|
| Substrates | Bulky, helix-distorting lesions | Small base modifications (no helix distortion) |
| Excision size | ~12–13 nt (bacteria) / ~25–30 nt (euk) | 1 base + 1–2 nt |
| Initiation | Helix distortion sensing | Specific glycosylase recognizes base |

---

## Common Exam Traps
- NER does **not** only fix UV damage — any helix-distorting lesion qualifies
- The two UvrC incision sites are **not** equidistant from the lesion (8 nt 3', 4–5 nt 5')
- NER excises an **oligonucleotide**; BER excises just the **base**
