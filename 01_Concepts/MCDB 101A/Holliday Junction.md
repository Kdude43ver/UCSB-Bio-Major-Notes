# Holliday Junction and Recombination Models

**Topic Area:** Recombination
**Lecture:** [[Recombination]]
**Exam:** Final
**Importance:** MEDIUM-HIGH YIELD ⭐⭐

---

## One-Line Definition
The Holliday junction is a four-way DNA intermediate formed during homologous recombination; it can migrate (branch migration) and be resolved in two orientations to produce either crossover or non-crossover (patch) products.

---

## Holliday Model (1964) — Steps
1. Two homologous duplexes align in parallel
2. Single-strand nicks at equivalent positions
3. Strand exchange → **Holliday junction** (4-way junction)
4. **Branch migration** (RuvA/RuvB) extends heteroduplex region
5. **Resolution** by RuvC (cuts at 5'-A↓TTG-3'):
   - Splice (crossover): cuts strands NOT originally exchanged → flanking markers recombine
   - Patch (non-crossover): cuts strands that WERE exchanged → flanking markers parental; only heteroduplex patch remains

---

## DSB Repair Model (Szostak, 1983) — Key Differences
- Initiated by a **double-strand break** (not simultaneous nicks) — more biologically realistic
- 5'→3' resection leaves 3' ssDNA tails → strand invasion → D-loop → two HJs form
- Predicts **gene conversion** at the break site (broken molecule is repaired using intact homolog as template)
- Better supported experimentally

---

## RuvABC System

| Protein | Role |
|---------|------|
| RuvA | Recognizes HJ; recruits RuvB |
| RuvB | AAA+ ATPase; drives branch migration |
| RuvC | Resolvase; sequence-specific cuts to resolve HJ |

---

## Gene Conversion
Arises when heteroduplex DNA containing a mismatch is repaired using one strand as template → both daughter chromatids carry one parent's sequence at that site. Evidence: 3:1 or 1:3 segregation ratios in fungal tetrads.

---

## Common Exam Traps (from NLM)
- The original Holliday model uses **single-strand nicks**, not DSBs
- Resolution orientation (splice vs. patch) determines whether flanking markers recombine — draw both to get full credit
- Gene conversion is NOT the same as crossover — it can occur without exchange of flanking markers
