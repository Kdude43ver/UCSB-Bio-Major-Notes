# Meselson-Stahl Experiment

**Topic Area:** DNA Replication
**Lecture:** [[DNA Replication]]
**Exam:** Midterm 1
**Importance:** HIGH YIELD ⭐⭐⭐

---

## One-Line Definition
The Meselson-Stahl experiment (1958) used density-gradient centrifugation of ¹⁵N/¹⁴N-labeled *E. coli* DNA to definitively prove that DNA replication is **semiconservative** — each daughter molecule retains one parental strand.

---

## Experimental Design
1. Grow *E. coli* in ¹⁵N medium until all DNA is heavy (¹⁵N/¹⁵N)
2. Transfer to ¹⁴N (light) medium
3. Harvest samples after 0, 1, 2, 3 generations
4. Extract DNA; separate by **CsCl equilibrium density gradient centrifugation**
5. Bands visualized by UV absorption at 260 nm

---

## Results

| Generation | Band position | Interpretation |
|-----------|--------------|---------------|
| 0 | One heavy band | All ¹⁵N/¹⁵N |
| 1 | **One intermediate band** | All ¹⁵N/¹⁴N — one old + one new strand |
| 2 | Intermediate + light band (1:1) | Half hybrid, half light |
| 3 | Mostly light + some intermediate (1:3 ratio) | Exponential dilution of hybrid |

---

## Why This Rules Out Alternatives

| Model | Predicted Gen-1 result | Observed? |
|-------|----------------------|-----------|
| **Conservative** | Two bands: heavy + light | ❌ No |
| **Dispersive** | Single band, continuously lightening | ❌ No |
| **Semiconservative** | Single **intermediate** band | ✅ Yes |

---

## Key Technical Point
CsCl density gradient separates DNA molecules by **buoyant density**. ¹⁵N/¹⁵N DNA is denser than ¹⁵N/¹⁴N hybrid, which is denser than ¹⁴N/¹⁴N — three distinguishable positions.
