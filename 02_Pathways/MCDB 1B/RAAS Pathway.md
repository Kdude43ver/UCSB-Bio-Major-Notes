# RAAS — Renin-Angiotensin-Aldosterone System Pathway

**Course:** [[Course Dashboard]] | **Yield:** MEDIUM ⭐⭐

---

## Pathway Overview

The RAAS is a hormonal cascade that maintains blood pressure and fluid balance. It is activated by low blood pressure, low blood sodium, or sympathetic stimulation, and leads to vasoconstriction and increased sodium retention.

---

## Step-by-Step Pathway

```
TRIGGER: ↓ Blood pressure, ↓ Na⁺ delivery to macula densa, or ↑ sympathetic stimulation
         (β₁ adrenergic receptors on JG cells)
        ↓
KIDNEY — Juxtaglomerular (JG) cells secrete RENIN
        ↓
Renin cleaves ANGIOTENSINOGEN (liver protein, always in blood)
        ↓
ANGIOTENSIN I (10 amino acids, inactive)
        ↓
ACE (Angiotensin Converting Enzyme) — in LUNG endothelium
        ↓
ANGIOTENSIN II (8 amino acids, highly active)
        ↓
        ├── Vasoconstriction of arterioles → ↑ Total Peripheral Resistance → ↑ BP
        │
        ├── → Adrenal cortex (zona glomerulosa) → ALDOSTERONE released
        │          ↓
        │   Kidney DCT and Collecting Duct
        │          ↓
        │   ↑ ENaC (epithelial Na⁺ channels) expression
        │   ↑ Na⁺/K⁺ ATPase on basolateral side
        │          ↓
        │   ↑ Na⁺ reabsorption → ↑ water follows (osmosis) → ↑ blood volume → ↑ BP
        │   ↑ K⁺ secretion → K⁺ excreted in urine
        │
        ├── → Posterior pituitary → ↑ ADH secretion → ↑ water reabsorption (AQP-2)
        │
        └── → Hypothalamus → ↑ thirst → ↑ water intake
```

---

## Outcomes

| Effect | Mechanism | Result |
|--------|-----------|--------|
| Vasoconstriction | Ang II → smooth muscle contraction | ↑ BP immediately |
| Na⁺ retention | Aldosterone → ENaC, Na⁺/K⁺ ATPase | ↑ blood volume → ↑ BP (slow) |
| Water retention | ADH → AQP-2 in CD | ↑ blood volume |
| K⁺ excretion | Aldosterone → K⁺ secretion | Hypokalemia risk if RAAS overactive |

---

## Pharmacological Targets

| Drug class | Target | Effect |
|------------|--------|--------|
| ACE inhibitors (e.g., lisinopril) | ACE | ↓ Ang II → ↓ BP |
| ARBs (e.g., losartan) | Ang II receptor | Block Ang II effect → ↓ BP |
| Aldosterone antagonists (e.g., spironolactone) | Mineralocorticoid receptor | ↓ Na⁺ retention → ↓ BP, K⁺ sparing |
| β-blockers | β₁ receptor on JG cells | ↓ Renin release → ↓ RAAS activation |

---

## Counter-regulatory: ANP

Atrial Natriuretic Peptide (from distended atria) **opposes RAAS**:
- ↑ GFR → ↑ filtration
- ↓ Renin and aldosterone secretion
- ↑ Na⁺ excretion (natriuresis) → ↓ blood volume → ↓ BP
