# Synaptic Transmission Pathway

**Course:** [[Course Dashboard]] | **Yield:** HIGH ⭐⭐⭐

---

## Pathway Overview

Synaptic transmission converts an electrical signal (action potential) into a chemical signal (neurotransmitter release) and back into an electrical signal (postsynaptic potential). This occurs at specialized junctions called synapses.

---

## Step-by-Step Pathway

```
ACTION POTENTIAL arrives at presynaptic terminal
        ↓
Membrane depolarization opens VOLTAGE-GATED Ca²⁺ CHANNELS
        ↓
Ca²⁺ flows into presynaptic terminal (↑[Ca²⁺]ᵢ)
        ↓
Ca²⁺ binds SYNAPTOTAGMIN on synaptic vesicle
        ↓
SNARE complex forms (synaptobrevin + syntaxin + SNAP-25)
        ↓
Vesicle docks and fuses with presynaptic membrane
        ↓
NEUROTRANSMITTERS released into synaptic cleft (exocytosis)
        ↓
NT diffuses across 20–40 nm cleft
        ↓
NT binds POSTSYNAPTIC RECEPTORS
    ├── Ionotropic (ligand-gated ion channels) → fast, direct
    │       ↓
    │   Ion channels open immediately
    │   EPSP: Na⁺ in → depolarization
    │   IPSP: Cl⁻ in or K⁺ out → hyperpolarization
    │
    └── Metabotropic (GPCRs) → slow, amplified
            ↓
        G protein activated → second messenger cascade
        (cAMP → PKA, or IP₃/DAG → PKC)
            ↓
        Downstream effects (modulate channels, gene expression)

CLEARANCE of neurotransmitter:
    ├── Reuptake by presynaptic terminal (transporters)
    ├── Enzymatic degradation (e.g., AChE breaks ACh)
    └── Diffusion away from cleft
```

---

## Integration at Axon Hillock

```
Multiple inputs arrive simultaneously or in rapid succession
        ↓
EPSPs and IPSPs summate:
   ├── Temporal summation: rapid firing of same input
   └── Spatial summation: simultaneous inputs from multiple synapses
        ↓
If net depolarization reaches threshold (~−55 mV)
        ↓
ACTION POTENTIAL generated at axon hillock → propagates down axon
```

## Key Molecules

| Molecule | Role | Notes |
|----------|------|-------|
| Ca²⁺ | Trigger for vesicle fusion | Enters via N-type or P/Q-type VGCCs |
| Synaptotagmin | Ca²⁺ sensor | Binds Ca²⁺ → triggers SNARE |
| SNARE proteins | Vesicle docking/fusion | Target of botulinum and tetanus toxins |
| Acetylcholine | NT (NMJ, PNS) | Degraded by AChE |
| Glutamate | Excitatory NT (CNS) | AMPA, NMDA, kainate receptors |
| GABA | Inhibitory NT (CNS) | Cl⁻ influx → hyperpolarization |

---

## Pharmacological Relevance

| Drug | Mechanism | Effect |
|------|-----------|--------|
| Curare | Blocks nicotinic ACh receptors | Muscle paralysis |
| Botulinum toxin | Cleaves SNARE proteins | Blocks NT release → paralysis |
| Tetanus toxin | Cleaves SNARE in inhibitory interneurons | Spastic paralysis |
| SSRIs | Block serotonin reuptake | ↑ synaptic serotonin |
| Benzodiazepines | Enhance GABA-A receptor | ↑ Cl⁻ influx → sedation |
