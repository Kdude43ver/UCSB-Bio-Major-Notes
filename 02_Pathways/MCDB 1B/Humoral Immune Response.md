# Pathway: Humoral (Antibody-Mediated) Immune Response

**Type:** Adaptive immunity | **System:** Immune System | **Module:** [[Immune System]]

---

## Full Pathway

```
PATHOGEN ENTERS (e.g., bacterium)
       ↓
INNATE RESPONSE FIRST:
  Macrophage/dendritic cell PHAGOCYTOSES pathogen
       ↓
Pathogen peptides displayed on MHC CLASS II
       ↓
HELPER T CELL (CD4⁺) recognizes MHC II + antigen
  (via T cell receptor, TCR)
       ↓
Helper T cell ACTIVATED → releases cytokines
       ↓
Cytokines activate B CELLS (that recognize same antigen)
       ↓
B cells undergo CLONAL EXPANSION (rapid division)
       ↓
B cells differentiate into:
  ├── PLASMA CELLS → secrete ANTIBODIES (thousands/second)
  └── MEMORY B CELLS → long-lived; enable faster future response
       ↓
ANTIBODIES:
  - Bind and neutralize pathogen (block attachment)
  - Opsonize pathogen (flag for macrophage destruction)
  - Activate complement system
  - Agglutinate pathogens
```

---

## Cellular (CTL) Immune Response (parallel pathway)

```
VIRUS-INFECTED CELL presents viral peptides on MHC CLASS I
       ↓
CYTOTOXIC T CELL (CD8⁺, CTL) recognizes MHC I + antigen
       ↓
CTL undergoes CLONAL EXPANSION (activation phase)
       ↓
EFFECTOR PHASE:
  CTLs contact infected cell
       ↓
CTL releases PERFORIN → punches holes in infected cell membrane
       ↓
LYSIS of infected cell → virus eliminated
       ↓
Memory CTLs form for future responses
```

---

## Summary: Which T Cell Does What?

| Cell | Marker | Recognizes | Function |
|---|---|---|---|
| Helper T cell | CD4⁺ | MHC Class II (on APCs) | Activates B cells and CTLs; orchestrates response |
| Cytotoxic T cell | CD8⁺ | MHC Class I (on all nucleated cells) | Kills infected/cancer cells via perforin |

---

## Exam Checkpoints ✅

- [ ] MHC II activates Helper T cells (CD4⁺)
- [ ] MHC I activates Cytotoxic T cells (CD8⁺)
- [ ] Plasma cells produce antibodies (B cells differentiate into plasma cells)
- [ ] Memory cells = basis for vaccine immunity
- [ ] Perforin = weapon of cytotoxic T cells (not antibodies)
- [ ] Transplant rejection = CTLs attack foreign MHC I on donor organ
