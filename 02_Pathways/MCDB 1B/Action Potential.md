# Pathway: Action Potential

**Type:** Electrical signaling | **System:** Nervous System | **Module:** [[Nervous System]]

---

## Step-by-Step Pathway

```
RESTING STATE (−70 mV)
       ↓
Stimulus reaches threshold (~−55 mV)
       ↓
Voltage-gated Na⁺ channels OPEN
       ↓
Na⁺ rushes INTO cell (high to low [Na⁺]; following electrical gradient)
       ↓
Membrane depolarizes: −70 mV → +50 mV
       ↓
Na⁺ channels INACTIVATE (cannot open again immediately)
Voltage-gated K⁺ channels OPEN
       ↓
K⁺ rushes OUT of cell (following concentration gradient)
       ↓
Membrane repolarizes: +50 mV → −70 mV
       ↓
K⁺ channels SLOW to close → brief hyperpolarization (below −70 mV)
       ↓
Na⁺/K⁺ pump restores gradients (3 Na⁺ out, 2 K⁺ in, uses ATP)
       ↓
RESTING STATE restored (−70 mV)
```

---

## What Triggers This Downstream

```
Action potential reaches axon terminal
       ↓
Voltage-gated Ca²⁺ channels open
       ↓
Ca²⁺ enters terminal
       ↓
Vesicles fuse with presynaptic membrane
       ↓
Neurotransmitters released into synaptic cleft
       ↓
Bind postsynaptic receptors → EPSP or IPSP
```

---

## Exam Checkpoints ✅

- [ ] Na⁺ rushes IN during depolarization (not out)
- [ ] K⁺ rushes OUT during repolarization (not in)
- [ ] Na⁺/K⁺ pump pumps Na⁺ OUT and K⁺ IN (against gradients, uses ATP)
- [ ] All-or-nothing: must reach threshold
- [ ] Absolute refractory period: Na⁺ channels inactivated — cannot re-fire
