# Ovarian Cycle Pathway

**Course:** [[Course Dashboard]] | **Yield:** MEDIUM ⭐⭐

---

## Pathway Overview

The ovarian cycle is a 28-day hormonal feedback loop coordinating follicle development, ovulation, and the luteal phase. It involves the hypothalamo-pituitary-gonadal (HPG) axis and switches between negative and positive feedback.

---

## Pathway Diagram

```
HYPOTHALAMUS
GnRH (pulsatile)
        ↓
ANTERIOR PITUITARY
FSH + LH
        ↓
        ├── FSH → Follicle development (days 1–13)
        │         ↓
        │   Granulosa cells produce ESTROGEN (↑↑ by day 12–13)
        │
        └── LH → low at first (baseline)

ESTROGEN FEEDBACK:
Early (low estrogen): Negative feedback → suppresses FSH/LH
Late (high estrogen, day 12-13): Positive feedback → TRIGGERS LH SURGE

                ─── LH SURGE (day 13) ───
                        ↓
              OVULATION (day 14)
              Dominant follicle ruptures → releases oocyte
                        ↓
              Ruptured follicle → CORPUS LUTEUM
                        ↓
              Corpus luteum secretes PROGESTERONE + estrogen
                        ↓
              Prepares endometrium for implantation
              Negative feedback → FSH and LH suppressed
                        ↓
              If NO fertilization (day ~25–28):
              Corpus luteum degenerates → progesterone/estrogen drop
                        ↓
              FSH rises again → new cycle begins
              Endometrium shed → MENSTRUATION

              If FERTILIZATION:
              Embryo implants → secretes hCG
              hCG maintains corpus luteum → progesterone sustained
              No menstruation
```

---

## Hormone Levels Through the Cycle

| Phase | FSH | LH | Estrogen | Progesterone |
|-------|-----|-----|---------|--------------|
| Menstruation (d1–5) | Rising | Low | Low | Low |
| Follicular (d6–13) | Elevated | Low | Rising | Low |
| Ovulation (d14) | Peak then drop | Surge | Peak | Low |
| Luteal (d15–28) | Low | Low | Moderate | High |
| Pre-menstruation (d26–28) | Rising | Rising | Dropping | Dropping |

---

## Key Feedback Switch

- **Low estrogen** → **negative feedback** on GnRH/LH/FSH
- **High estrogen (>200 pg/mL for >50 h)** → **positive feedback** → LH surge → ovulation
- **Progesterone** → always negative feedback → prevents new follicle development during luteal phase
