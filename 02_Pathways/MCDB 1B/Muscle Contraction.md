# Pathway: Muscle Contraction

**Type:** Mechanochemical | **System:** Muscular/Nervous | **Module:** [[Muscular System]]

---

## Full Pathway (Neural → Molecular)

```
Motor neuron action potential arrives at neuromuscular junction
       ↓
Acetylcholine (ACh) released into synaptic cleft
       ↓
ACh binds nicotinic receptors on muscle fiber sarcolemma
       ↓
Na⁺ enters → depolarization → action potential in muscle fiber
       ↓
Action potential travels along T-tubules (deep into fiber)
       ↓
Ca²⁺ released from sarcoplasmic reticulum (SR)
       ↓
Ca²⁺ binds TROPONIN (on actin filament)
       ↓
Tropomyosin shifts → myosin-binding sites on actin EXPOSED
       ↓
───── CROSS-BRIDGE CYCLE ─────
Myosin head (low-energy) binds actin
       ↓
ATP binds myosin → myosin RELEASES actin
       ↓
ATP hydrolyzed (ADP + Pᵢ) → myosin moves to high-energy (cocked) position
       ↓
Myosin re-attaches to NEW site on actin
       ↓
ADP + Pᵢ released → POWER STROKE (sarcomere shortens)
       ↓
Cycle repeats while Ca²⁺ and ATP present
       ↓
─── RELAXATION ───
Ca²⁺ actively pumped back into SR (requires ATP)
       ↓
Tropomyosin re-covers myosin-binding sites
       ↓
Muscle RELAXES
```

---

## "What If?" Disruptions (Exam-Ready)

| Disruption | Result |
|---|---|
| Troponin cannot bind Ca²⁺ | Tropomyosin stays blocking → NO contraction |
| Ca²⁺ pump stops working | Ca²⁺ stays elevated → sustained uncontrolled contraction |
| No ATP | Myosin cannot release actin → muscle locked (rigor) |
| ACh receptor blocked (e.g., curare) | No muscle action potential → paralysis |

---

## Exam Checkpoints ✅

- [ ] ATP binds at Step 2 (to release myosin from actin) — NOT to power the stroke
- [ ] Power stroke at Step 4 (when ADP + Pᵢ released)
- [ ] Ca²⁺ from SR (not extracellular)
- [ ] Ca²⁺ binds TROPONIN (not tropomyosin directly)
- [ ] Relaxation requires ATP (for SR Ca²⁺ pump)
