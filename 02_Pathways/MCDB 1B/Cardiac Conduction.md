# Pathway: Cardiac Conduction & ECG

**Type:** Electrical | **System:** Circulatory | **Module:** [[Circulatory System]]

---

## Electrical Conduction Pathway

```
SA NODE (sinoatrial node) — spontaneous pacemaker
       ↓
Wave of depolarization spreads across BOTH ATRIA
       ↓
ATRIA CONTRACT (P wave on ECG)
       ↓
Signal reaches AV NODE (atrioventricular node)
       ↓
BRIEF DELAY (~0.1 sec) — allows ventricles to fill with blood
       ↓
Signal travels down BUNDLE OF HIS (interventricular septum)
       ↓
Spreads through PURKINJE FIBERS (throughout ventricular walls)
       ↓
VENTRICLES CONTRACT simultaneously from apex upward (QRS complex on ECG)
       ↓
Ventricles REPOLARIZE (T wave on ECG)
       ↓
Ventricles relax → diastole begins
       ↓
Cycle repeats (~70 times/min at rest)
```

---

## ECG Waveforms

| Wave | Event | Clinical note |
|---|---|---|
| **P wave** | Atrial depolarization | Small wave; atria contract |
| **QRS complex** | Ventricular depolarization | Larger; ventricles contract |
| **T wave** | Ventricular repolarization | Rounded wave; ventricles relax |

> Atrial repolarization is hidden within the QRS complex.

---

## Blood Flow Pathway (correlated)

```
Deoxygenated blood:
Body → Vena cavae → Right atrium → (tricuspid valve) → Right ventricle
→ Pulmonary artery → Lungs (gas exchange)

Oxygenated blood:
Lungs → Pulmonary veins → Left atrium → (mitral valve) → Left ventricle
→ Aorta → Body
```

---

## Exam Checkpoints ✅

- [ ] SA node is the pacemaker (not AV node)
- [ ] AV node introduces delay (ventricular filling time)
- [ ] P wave = atrial contraction; QRS = ventricular contraction; T = ventricular repolarization
- [ ] Left ventricle wall thicker (must overcome greater systemic resistance)
- [ ] Pulmonary veins carry OXYGENATED blood (veins = to heart; arteries = from heart — regardless of O₂ content)
