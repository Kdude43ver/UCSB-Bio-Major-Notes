## Answer Key

| Q | Answer | Explanation |
|---|--------|-------------|
| 1 | **B** | The σ subunit is essential for initiating transcription by recognizing the −10 and −35 promoter elements, directing the core RNA polymerase to the correct site. |
| 2 | **B** | Rho-independent terminators consist of an inverted repeat forming a hairpin in the RNA, followed by a poly-U tail, causing RNA polymerase to dissociate from the DNA. |
| 3 | **B** | Degeneracy means that there are 64 possible codons but only 20 amino acids — most amino acids are encoded by multiple synonymous codons. |
| 4 | **C** | Wobble base-pairing occurs at the 3' end of the codon (= 5' end of the anticodon), allowing one tRNA to recognize multiple synonymous codons differing at position 3. |
| 5 | **B** | The P-site holds the tRNA carrying the nascent polypeptide chain. The A-site accepts incoming aminoacyl-tRNAs; the E-site holds exiting deacylated tRNAs. |
| 6 | **C** | Glucose levels inversely affect cAMP concentration. Because CAP requires cAMP to bind the promoter and activate transcription, high glucose prevents full operon induction. |
| 7 | **B** | When [Trp] is high, the ribosome moves quickly through the leader peptide, allowing the 3-4 termination hairpin to form. When [Trp] is low, the ribosome stalls at Trp codons, allowing the 2-3 antiterminator hairpin to form instead. |
| 8 | **C** | CI (lambda repressor) maintains lysogeny by repressing lytic promoters (P_L and P_R) while activating its own maintenance promoter P_RM via positive autoregulation. |
| 9 | **B** | CI dimers bind cooperatively to O_R1 and O_R2; binding at the high-affinity O_R1 site facilitates binding at O_R2, collectively repressing P_R and activating P_RM. |
| 10 | **C** | DNA damage triggers the SOS response → RecA activation → RecA stimulates proteolytic cleavage (autocleavage) of CI → CI can no longer bind operators → lytic genes are derepressed. |
