## Questions

**1.** You perform a complementation test (trans test) between two independent phage mutants that exhibit the same mutant phenotype. If the co-infection fails to produce any wild-type progeny on a restrictive host, what is the most likely conclusion?

A. The two mutations are in different genes.
B. The two mutations are in the same gene.
C. The two mutations are separated by a large physical distance.
D. One of the mutations is a deletion and the other is a point mutation.

---

**2.** In a linear biosynthetic pathway (X → A → B → C → Phe), a mutant is unable to grow on minimal media supplemented with intermediate A but can grow if supplemented with B or C. Which step is most likely blocked in this mutant?

A. X to A
B. A to B
C. B to C
D. C to Phe

---

**3.** You are mapping the location of point mutations using a series of overlapping deletions. Point mutation 'd' fails to produce wild-type recombinants when crossed with Deletion 1 but successfully produces them when crossed with Deletions 2 and 3. What does this indicate about the location of mutation 'd'?

A. It is located within the region covered by Deletion 1.
B. It is located within the region covered by Deletion 2.
C. It is located in a region where all three deletions overlap.
D. It is not located on the same chromosome as the deletions.

**4.** If one strand of a double-stranded DNA molecule has an (A+G)/(T+C) ratio of 0.4, what is the (A+G)/(T+C) ratio of the complementary strand?

A. 0.4
B. 0.6
C. 2.5
D. 1.0

**5.** A covalently closed circular DNA (cccDNA) molecule of 1000 bp is fully relaxed with a helical pitch of 10 bp/turn. If the pitch is changed to 12.5 bp/turn without breaking the sugar-phosphate backbone, what will be the resulting writhe (W)?

A. +20
B. −20
C. +100
D. 0

**6.** Which of the following DNA sequences would have the highest melting temperature (Tm)?

B. A sequence with 70% G-C content in low salt concentration.
C. A sequence with 30% G-C content in high salt concentration.
D. A sequence with 70% G-C content in high salt concentration.

**7.** In the Meselson-Stahl experiment, what band pattern would be observed in a cesium chloride gradient after **two** generations of semiconservative replication in ¹⁴N medium (following many generations in ¹⁵N)?

B. One intermediate band (¹⁵N/¹⁴N).
C. One intermediate band and one light band (¹⁴N/¹⁴N).

## Answer Key

| Q | Answer | Explanation |
|---|--------|-------------|
| 1 | **B** | Failure to complement (no growth/plaques on restrictive host) means the cell lacks a functional copy of at least one essential gene product — mutations are allelic (in the same gene). |
| 2 | **B** | A mutant grows if supplemented with any metabolite downstream of the block. Grows on B and C but not A → block is between A and B. |
| 3 | **A** | Recombination cannot produce wild-type if the point mutation falls within the deleted region. Failure to recombine with Deletion 1 places it inside Deletion 1's interval. |
| 4 | **C** | By Chargaff's rules and base-pairing, the purine/pyrimidine ratio on one strand is the reciprocal of the ratio on the complementary strand: 1/0.4 = 2.5. |
| 5 | **A** | L is invariant in cccDNA. Initially L = T + W = (1000/10) + 0 = 100. With new pitch: T = 1000/12.5 = 80. So W = 100 − 80 = **+20**. |
| 6 | **D** | Tm increases with higher G-C content (3 H-bonds vs. 2 for A-T) and with high salt (shields phosphate repulsion). Both conditions maximize Tm. |
| 7 | **C** | After Gen 1: all hybrid (intermediate). After Gen 2: hybrid molecules produce one hybrid (¹⁵N/¹⁴N) and one light (¹⁴N/¹⁴N) per molecule → two bands. |
| 8 | **C** | The leading strand moves toward the fork (continuous); the lagging strand moves away and is made in Okazaki fragments. Both strands need RNA primers. |
| 9 | **C** | The 3'→5' exonuclease of Pol III allows the polymerase to "back up" and excise a mismatched base before continuing synthesis. |
| 10 | **B** | DnaA binds repeated 9-mer sequences at *oriC*; this triggers melting of the 13-mer repeats, creating the ssDNA required for helicase (DnaB) loading. |
