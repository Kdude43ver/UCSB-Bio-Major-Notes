## Section 2: True/False

**13.** The proximal convoluted tubule (PCT) is responsible for reabsorbing essentially all glucose from the filtrate.
**TRUE**

**14.** ADH causes the kidneys to produce more urine (diuresis).
**FALSE** — ADH increases water reabsorption → less, more concentrated urine.

**15.** Transplant rejection occurs because the recipient's cytotoxic T cells recognize foreign MHC Class I on the donor organ as non-self.
**TRUE**

**16.** Steroid hormones act faster than peptide hormones because they can cross the plasma membrane.
**FALSE** — Steroid hormones cross the membrane but act MORE SLOWLY (hours–days) because they alter gene expression. Peptide hormones act faster (seconds–minutes) via second messengers.

**17.** Memory B cells are responsible for the faster, stronger immune response upon re-exposure to a pathogen.
**TRUE**

## Section 3: Short Answer

**18.** A patient is diagnosed with diabetes insipidus — a condition where ADH is not produced. Predict the effects on kidney function and describe what symptoms the patient would experience. (3 points)

*Model Answer:* Without ADH, the collecting duct lacks aquaporins → water cannot be reabsorbed → urine remains dilute and large volumes are produced (up to 10–15 L/day). The patient would experience excessive urination (polyuria) and extreme thirst (polydipsia) as the body becomes dehydrated. Blood osmolarity would rise (hypernatremia) because water is being lost.

---

**19.** Compare and contrast the innate and adaptive immune systems. Include at least 3 differences and one example of each. (4 points)

*Model Answer:* Innate immunity is rapid (immediate response), non-specific (recognizes general pathogen patterns/PAMPs), has no memory, and includes components like macrophages, neutrophils, NK cells, and complement. Example: macrophage engulfs bacteria immediately upon infection. Adaptive immunity is slower (days–weeks on first exposure), highly specific (each B/T cell recognizes one antigen), has long-term memory, and includes B cells, T cells, and antibodies. Example: B cells produce specific antibodies against a particular virus epitope. Both work together — innate response buys time while adaptive response develops and provides lasting protection.
