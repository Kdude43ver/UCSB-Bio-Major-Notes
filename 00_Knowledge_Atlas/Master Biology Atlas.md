## 🧬 MCDB 101A — Molecular Genetics I

### Central Dogma Chain
```
DNA ──[Replication]──→ DNA
DNA ──[Transcription]──→ mRNA ──[Translation]──→ Protein
```

### Core Concept Map

**DNA Structure & Replication**
- [[DNA Structure]] — double helix, base pairing, antiparallel strands, Chargaff's rules
- [[DNA Replication]] — semi-conservative, bidirectional, leading/lagging strand, Okazaki fragments
- [[DNA Topology]] — supercoiling, topoisomerases, linking number
- [[Meselson-Stahl Experiment]] — proof of semi-conservative replication
- [[Okazaki Fragments]] — discontinuous synthesis, primase, Pol I gap fill, ligase

**Transcription & RNA**
- [[Transcription]] — RNAP, sigma factor, -10/-35 boxes, rho-independent/dependent termination
- [[Transcription Attenuation]] — trp operon, ribosome stalling, alternative hairpins
- [[Transcriptional Regulation]] — lac operon, operators, inducers, repressors, cis vs trans

**Translation & Protein**
- [[Translation]] — ribosome (30S/50S), codons, tRNA, aminoacyl-tRNA synthetases, A/P/E sites
- [[Genetic Code]] — degenerate, non-overlapping, universal, wobble hypothesis

**Gene Regulation**
- [[Lac Operon]] — inducible, dual control (CRP-cAMP positive; LacI repressor negative)
- [[Catabolite Repression]] — CAP/CRP system, glucose effect, cAMP levels
- [[Bacteriophage Lambda]] — lytic vs lysogenic switch, CI repressor, Cro, cooperative binding
- [[Transcription Attenuation]] — trp operon, alternative hairpin structures

**Mutation & Repair**
- [[Mutations]] — point mutations (transitions/transversions), frameshifts, Ames test
- [[Luria-Delbrück Fluctuation Test]] — pre-existing vs induced mutation hypothesis
- [[DNA Repair Pathways]] — overview of all four pathways
- [[Base Excision Repair]] — glycosylase removes damaged base; AP site; Pol I fills
- [[Nucleotide Excision Repair]] — bulky adducts; excises 12–13 nt oligonucleotide
- [[Mismatch Repair]] — replication errors; methylation distinguishes strands; MutS/L/H
- [[DNA Repair]] — double-strand break repair (HR, NHEJ)

**Recombination & Gene Transfer**
- [[Recombination]] — chi sites, RecA, Holliday junction, branch migration
- [[Holliday Junction]] — intermediate of homologous recombination; resolution gives crossover or non-crossover
- [[Conjugation]] — F plasmid, pilus, rolling circle, HFr high-frequency recombination
- [[Hfr Conjugation Mapping]] — time-of-entry mapping, gene order determination
- [[Transduction]] — phage carries bacterial DNA; generalized vs specialized
- [[Plasmids]] — F, R, Col plasmids; incompatibility groups
- [[Gene Transfer]] — overview of all three mechanisms

**Classical Genetics**
- [[Classical Genetics]] — one gene–one enzyme; Beadle & Tatum; Neurospora
- [[Complementation Testing]] — cis-trans test; cistron definition
- [[Deletion Mapping]] — Benzer's rII system; fine-structure mapping

**Techniques**
- [[Molecular Techniques]] — gel electrophoresis, restriction enzymes, sequencing overview
- [[PCR]] — polymerase chain reaction; primers, cycles, Taq polymerase

## 🔗 Cross-Course Concept Connections

### DNA → Gene Expression → Physiology Chain
| MCDB 101A Concept | Link | MCDB 1B Application |
|-------------------|------|---------------------|
| Transcription + lac operon | Gene regulation | Steroid hormones activate transcription in physiology |
| Translation | Central dogma | All enzymes, receptors, channels in physiology are proteins |
| DNA Repair → Mutations | Molecular basis | Mutations → cancer; immune system detects abnormal cells (MHC I) |
| Gene Transfer (conjugation) | Horizontal gene transfer | Antibiotic resistance (clinical relevance to immune/infection) |

### Shared Fundamental Mechanisms
| Mechanism | 101A Context | 1B Context |
|-----------|-------------|-----------|
| Membrane potential | Not directly covered | Action potential, resting potential |
| Ion channels (Na+/K+, Ca2+) | Not covered | Action potential, synaptic transmission, cardiac muscle |
| ATP as energy currency | DNA replication, repair | Cross-bridge cycle, Na+/K+ ATPase, SERCA |
| Negative feedback | Repressor binding operator | Hormonal axis regulation (RAAS, HPG) |
| Signal transduction | GPCR/cAMP in lac operon (CRP) | Peptide hormones, phototransduction, guard cell signaling |
| Cooperative binding | Not covered | Hemoglobin O2 binding; CI repressor |
| Enzyme kinetics | Restriction enzymes, Pol III | RuBisCo (Km issue), lipase, digestive enzymes |
| Protein structure | Operators, repressors | Antibodies, Hb, myosin, troponin |

### Key Evolutionary/Conceptual Links
- **RuBisCo** (MCDB 1B) fixes CO2 using Mg2+ and evolved from ancestral carboxylase; connects to **enzyme evolution** from 101A
- **DNA repair** (101A) connects to **immune system** (1B) — damaged/mutated cells present altered peptides on MHC I → cytotoxic T cells eliminate them
- **Transcriptional regulation** (101A, lac operon) shares logic with **hormone receptor signaling** (1B) — both use ligand-induced conformational change to modulate gene expression
- **Biofilm/conjugation** (101A gene transfer) connects to **antibiotic resistance** → relevant to innate immunity lecture
- **Phage lambda lytic/lysogenic switch** (101A) is conceptually parallel to **apoptosis vs proliferation** decisions in immune cells (1B)
