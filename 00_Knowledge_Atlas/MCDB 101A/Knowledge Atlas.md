## Concept Importance Rankings

### HIGH YIELD (appears on ≥2 exams or heavily emphasized)
- [[Lac Operon]] — regulation by repressor + CRP/CAP, both negative and positive control
- [[DNA Replication]] — Meselson-Stahl, leading/lagging, proofreading enzymes
- [[Complementation Testing]] — defining genes functionally
- [[Bacteriophage Lambda]] — lysis/lysogeny switch, cI/Cro competition
- [[Nucleotide Excision Repair]] — UvrABC mechanism, damage recognition
- [[Mismatch Repair]] — MutS/MutL/MutH, strand discrimination
- [[Hfr Conjugation Mapping]] — interrupted mating, gene order
- [[Transcriptional Regulation]] — σ factors, promoter elements, attenuation

### MEDIUM YIELD
- [[DNA Topology]] — linking number, supercoiling, topoisomerases
- [[Genetic Code]] — codon table, wobble, suppressor tRNAs
- [[Luria-Delbrück Fluctuation Test]] — random vs. directed mutation
- [[Holliday Junction]] — branch migration, resolution
- [[Transduction]] — generalized vs. specialized
- [[PCR]] — Taq polymerase, primer design, thermocycling

### LOW YIELD
- [[Base Excision Repair]] — glycosylase, AP site, AP endonuclease
- [[Okazaki Fragments]] — primase, DNA Pol I gap-filling
- [[Deletion Mapping]] — fine-structure mapping in rII system

## Cross-Domain Links

- [[DNA Replication]] → [[Mismatch Repair]] (MutH recognizes hemimethylated GATC)
- [[Transcription]] → [[Translation]] (coupled in prokaryotes)
- [[Lac Operon]] → [[Catabolite Repression]] (CRP-cAMP positive control)
- [[Bacteriophage Lambda]] → [[Recombination]] (λ integrase, site-specific recombination)
- [[Mutations]] → [[DNA Repair Pathways]] (repair corrects spontaneous and induced mutations)
- [[Conjugation]] → [[Plasmids]] (F plasmid, F+ transfer)
